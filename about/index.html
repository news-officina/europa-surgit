About Europa Surgit

Europa Surgit follows what Europe builds, wins, discovers and starts, in science, space, industry, technology, defence, culture, sport and politics. A first step counts as much as a finished success, as long as it is called what it is.

It stands for one Europe: one market, one army, one voice, many nationalities. It is an independent publication and is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any party or movement.

Articles here are drafted with AI from published sources. An editor checks most of them before they appear; an article that went out unchecked says so. Illustrations are generated with AI. Europa Surgit is independent and speaks for no party or movement.
